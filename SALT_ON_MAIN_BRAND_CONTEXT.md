## 1. Brand Overview

Salt on Main is a modern wellness destination centered on restorative, experiential wellness services.

The brand should not be presented as only a salt room or only a halotherapy business. Halotherapy is a distinctive foundation of the experience, but the business also offers infrared salty sauna, red light therapy, massage therapy, signature reset experiences, and private wellness events.

### Core Brand Direction
**Salt. Sweat. Reset.**

### Desired Brand Feel
- Restorative
- Welcoming
- Premium but accessible
- Calm
- Modern
- Holistic
- Experiential
- Trustworthy
- Warm
- Polished

### Avoid
- Overly clinical language
- Exaggerated wellness promises
- Generic spa language
- Medical guarantees
- Disease-treatment claims
- Cure/prevention language
- Overcomplicated explanations
- Copy that makes the business sound like it offers only halotherapy

## 2. Positioning

Salt on Main should be positioned as a destination where guests can slow down, restore, and reset through a collection of wellness experiences.

A useful positioning line:

> Wellness experiences designed to help you slow down, restore, and reset.

The website should make it clear that Salt on Main offers:

**Salt Therapy • Infrared Sauna • Red Light Therapy • Massage • Signature Reset Experiences • Private Events**

The website is the education, positioning, trust, and conversion layer.

Booker is the transactional layer for exact service selection, current pricing, appointment availability, scheduling, and checkout.
